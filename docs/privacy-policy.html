MetroYatri privacy policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

In short

MetroYatri is an independent metro guide for Android (app ID com.metroyatri.delhi). It has no adverts and no tracking, and nothing about you is sold or shared for marketing. It keeps only what it needs to work: your account, your saved routes and your practice bookings.

Your account

When you create an account the app stores your name, your email address and a password. Accounts are handled by Firebase Authentication, a Google service. The password is kept by Google in a protected form and the developer cannot see it. Your email is used only to log you in, to send the verification link and to reset your password.

Saved routes and bookings

If your email is verified, your saved routes and practice bookings are also kept in Google Cloud Firestore under your own account, so they appear on any phone where you log in. Only you can read them. Practice bookings contain the stations, the time, the number of passengers and the fare. No payment details are ever collected, because no money is taken in this app. Bookings made in the app are for practice and are not valid metro tickets.

Your location

The app asks for your location only when you tap Nearest station or the locate button on the map. Your position is used on the phone to find the closest stations and to show a dot on the map. It is not stored and it is not sent to the developer or to any server.

Address search and street map

When you search for an address or landmark, the words you type are sent to the OpenStreetMap Nominatim search service to find the place. The street map pictures are downloaded from OpenStreetMap. Like any website, these services can see your internet address when the app contacts them. They are not told who you are.

Notifications

Arrival alerts and reminders are created on your phone. The app does not use a push notification server and does not collect a notification ID for your phone.

What stays only on your phone

Your profile photo, the city and language you chose, recent searches, the dark mode setting and reminder settings stay on the phone and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Deleting your account and data

In the app, open Account and tap Delete my account, then tap again to confirm. This removes your account from Firebase and deletes your saved routes and bookings from Firestore straight away. If you cannot open the app, ask for deletion through the contact below and give the email address of the account. Requests are handled within 30 days.

Children

The app is a travel guide for general use and is not aimed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is shown here and inside the app, with a new date.

Contact

MetroYatri is made by Rudra Gupta, an independent developer in India. For questions or a deletion request, open an issue on the project page.

MetroYatri is not made by, linked to or approved by any metro operator.
